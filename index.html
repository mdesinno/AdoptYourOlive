We are the de Sinno family. For over 70 years, we have cultivated ancient olive trees in the sunny countryside of San Severo.

When you adopt a tree, you are not just buying oil. You help us preserve traditional farming methods against intensive agriculture. In return, we share with you our most precious reserve: a Single Estate Extra Virgin Olive Oil, rich in history and flavor.

Everything you need to know about your adoption.

You have complete freedom to choose! During checkout, you can select Immediate Shipping to have your complete kit (EVOO supply + custom label + physical certificate) dispatched right away from our current family reserve.

Alternatively, choose Pre-order for New Harvest: you will instantly receive an official Digital Reservation Certificate via email (perfect for gifts!), and your complete physical box with the freshest olive oil will ship directly to your address in January. You will receive an address confirmation reminder before dispatch.

Geographic Note: Pre-orders include tracked delivery to the country selected at checkout (EU, UK, Switzerland, USA, or Canada).

Tracked shipping is 100% included for all destinations within the European Union. For non-EU countries, shipping rates are calculated at checkout based on destination. Please note that any import duties, customs clearance fees, or local taxes imposed by non-EU destination authorities are beyond our control and are strictly the responsibility of the recipient.

Our direct shipping network currently covers the European Union, UK, Switzerland, USA, and Canada. For deliveries to other destinations, shipment feasibility and rates must be verified with our team prior to dispatch.
